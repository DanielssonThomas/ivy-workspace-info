Privacy

Ivy accesses the Google Workspace data covered by the permissions you select on Google’s consent screen. It uses that data to provide the Workspace features you request and performs writes to Google services through the account you connect.

Ivy stores its OAuth credentials in the local Hermes profile. Information returned by Google can appear in local conversation history or tool records. When you use a hosted model in Ivy, information needed to fulfill the request can be sent to the configured model provider. The permissions you grant and the workflows you choose determine which data Ivy can access.

This information site does not receive your Google credentials or Workspace data. You can remove Ivy’s access under Google Account connections and ask the Ivy operator to remove locally retained conversation data. For questions about the integration, use the support contact on Ivy’s Google consent screen.
