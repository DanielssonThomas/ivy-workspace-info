Ivy Workspace

Ivy is a personal desktop assistant. Its Google Workspace connection helps a small group of people known to the operator work with their own Google accounts from Ivy.

When you authorize the connection, Ivy can use the Google services and permissions you select. These can cover calendar events, messages, contacts, files, documents and spreadsheets. Ivy uses this access for the work you request, such as finding an upcoming event, retrieving a document or sending an email you ask it to send.

You connect your account from the installed assistant. This site provides information about the connection.
